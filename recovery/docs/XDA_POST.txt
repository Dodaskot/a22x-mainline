[SIZE=5][B]What it does[/B][/SIZE]

[B]Android[/B]
[LIST]
[*]Samsung firmware straight from its ZIP, flashed as Odin does (without the bootloader, the modem and the PIT).
[*]GSIs from the built-in catalog, or a ROM from a file: system image, 7z, payload.bin, block OTA, image ZIP.
[*]Flashable ZIPs with their own installer, one or several in a row; [B]adb sideload[/B] works as in TWRP.
[*]Root with Magisk or APatch; Samsung's stock BOOT back from the firmware.
[*]Shows why Android did not start (from its last kernel log).
[/LIST]

[B]Linux[/B]
[LIST]
[*]Install Debian, Ubuntu, Fedora, Arch Linux ARM, Manjaro ARM or Mobian from the catalog over Wi-Fi, or from a file.
[*]Several systems side by side with a shared /home; switch between them.
[/LIST]

[B]Backup and restore[/B]
[LIST]
[*]Copies of Android's partitions, EFS and IMEI, the recovery itself, Linux snapshots.
[*]Copies can be protected with a password (they open with openssl on any computer).
[/LIST]

[B]Everything else[/B]
[LIST]
[*]Flash a partition (BOOT, DTBO, vbmeta, super and others), with the old content saved first.
[*]Files: browse, edit, archives, check and repair ext4, F2FS and FAT.
[*]Terminal, USB files (MTP), ADB and SSH over the USB cable, Wi-Fi.
[*]Updates of the recovery from inside the recovery.
[*]Six languages, three themes and your own from a file (see the Beaver example), 60/90 Hz, an optional password.
[/LIST]

[SIZE=5][B]New in 1.0.2[/B][/SIZE]
[LIST]
[*]The app writes Wi-Fi networks, language and time zone straight into the recovery's own settings in misc. The app and the recovery share one list of networks: for a network, the newer password wins, wherever it was set.
[/LIST]

[SIZE=5][B]New in 1.0.1[/B][/SIZE]
[LIST]
[*]The settings, the password and the saved Wi-Fi networks are kept in a free part of the [B]misc[/B] partition, twice, with checksums. A normal stock firmware flash does not touch them. Your settings from 1.0.0 are moved there by themselves at the first start.
[*]Many fixes from code reviews. No new features.
[/LIST]

[SIZE=5][B]Not available yet[/B][/SIZE]
[LIST]

[/LIST]

[SIZE=5][B]How to install[/B][/SIZE]
[LIST=1]
[*]Unlock the bootloader (this erases the phone).
[*]In Download Mode flash the image to RECOVERY, for example with Heimdall:
[CODE]heimdall flash --recovery recovery-a22x-1.0.2.img[/CODE]
Or install it from a rooted Android with the app: [URL=https://github.com/Dodaskot/mainline-recovery/releases/download/v1.0.2/mainline-recovery-installer-1.0.2.apk]mainline-recovery-installer-1.0.2.apk[/URL] (root needed).
[*]Start the recovery: phone off, USB cable to a computer, hold Volume up + Power.
[/LIST]
Already on 1.0.1, 1.0.0 or 0.6.x? Settings > Updates finds 1.0.2.
